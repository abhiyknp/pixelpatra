About Pixelpatra

Websites that people remember

पत्र (patra) means a page, or a letter. A website is both: a page people can visit and a letter from you to the world. Pixelpatra makes ready-made websites that read like a good letter and feel like a good room.

What we make

Portfolios, online shops, product websites and 3D experiences. Each one is designed once, tested on phones, and made so that its owner can run it day to day without a developer.

What we care about

  • Calm and fast. Pages that load quickly and respect people’s attention.
  • Immersive, but usable. A memorable first scroll should never get in the way of buying or contacting.
  • Yours to run. Owner views, locked brand details and plain files you can host anywhere.
  • Honest demos. Demos use sample data only, never a real customer’s information.

Made in India

Prices are in rupees, shops support UPI, GST invoices and Indian delivery, and every template works on the phones people actually use.
