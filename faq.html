FAQ

Questions, answered

Can’t find yours? Use the request form and we will reply within one working day (Monday to Saturday).

What do I get when I order a template?

In the Template files package you get the complete website files and a short guide. In Setup & launch we put your name, photos and text in and make it live for you. Custom build is for changes beyond that.

Can I try a template before I order?

Yes. Every template opens as a live demo with a control bar: switch the colour theme, type your name to see it as the brand, change device size, and open the Owner view. Nothing you try is stored anywhere except in your own browser.

Can I change colours, text and photos later?

Yes. Text and photos live in simple files, and templates with an Owner view let you change products, prices, text and offers without touching code.

What is the Owner view?

A private control panel inside the shop and portfolio templates where the owner manages day-to-day content. Brand name, tax and legal details are locked so they cannot be changed by mistake.

Do I need hosting or a domain?

You need a place to publish the files (many are free or cheap) and, if you want your own address, a domain. In Setup & launch we connect these for you.

Which devices and browsers are supported?

Current Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge on phones, tablets and computers. Templates are built phone first.

Who owns the website after I pay?

You do. You receive the files and can host them anywhere. Sample photos are replaced with your own; photos that stay in the demo carry their own licences, listed on the credits page.

How does payment work?

After you accept the quote we share payment details. Work starts, or files are sent, once payment is confirmed.

What if something does not work?

Tell us through the request form or reply to your quote. Defects in what we delivered are fixed free of charge for 30 days after delivery.

Can you build something that is not in the list?

Yes, choose Custom build in the request form and describe what you need. We will reply with a scope and a price.

Is my information safe?

The details you send in the request form are used only to reply to you. Your typed name and theme choice stay in your browser. Read the privacy page.
