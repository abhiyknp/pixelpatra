Privacy

Last updated 16 November 2026.

We collect as little as possible. This page explains what, and why.

Details you send us

If you use the request form, we receive the details you type (name, email or phone, business, what you need). We use them only to reply to you and prepare your quote, and keep them while we are working with you or until you ask us to delete them.

What stays in your browser

The name you type into the home page box, your colour theme choice, and anything you try in a live demo (cart, orders, Owner view edits) are saved in your own browser only. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data, or using “Reset demos” in the demo picker, removes them.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking. If we add visitor statistics, we will say so here.

Third parties

Page fonts load from Google Fonts. If you choose to contact us on WhatsApp or email, those services handle your message under their own policies.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the details you sent. Use the request form and say so.
