Privacy

Last updated 18 November 2026.

Pixelpatra operates online only from Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India. We collect as little as possible. This page explains what, why, and your choices, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

What we collect

If you use the request form, we receive what you type: your name, email or phone, business, what you need, budget and timeline. If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we receive your message and number. We do not ask for card or bank details on this website.

Why we use it

To reply to you, prepare your quote, deliver what you buy, and keep the records the law requires. We do not send marketing unless you ask for it, and we never sell your details.

What stays in your browser

The name you type into the home page box, your colour theme choice, and anything you try in a live demo (cart, orders, Owner view edits) are saved in your own browser only. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data, or using “Reset demos” in the demo picker, removes them.

Who handles it

Only the services needed to run the site: our web host and form service (Netlify, when the request form is used on the Netlify site), email and WhatsApp for our replies, and a payment provider when you pay. Page fonts load from Google Fonts. Each handles data under its own policy, and some may store data on servers outside India.

How long we keep it

Request details are kept while we work with you and for 12 months after our last contact, then deleted. Records of paid orders are kept for 8 years, as Indian tax law requires.

Children

This service is for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal data of a child.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking. If we add visitor statistics, we will say so here first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details you sent us, withdraw consent, or name someone to act for you. Use the request form and say so; we reply within one working day (Monday to Saturday) and finish within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

If something goes wrong

If a breach of your personal data affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
