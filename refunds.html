Refunds

Last updated 17 November 2026.

We want you to be happy with what you buy. Here is how refunds work.

Template files

Template files are digital and delivered immediately. Once delivered they cannot be returned, so they are not refundable, except where they do not work as described and we cannot fix the problem. In that case we will fix it, or refund you.

Setup & launch and Custom build

If you cancel before work begins, you receive a full refund. If you cancel after work has begun, you pay for the work already done. Once we have delivered the site and you have approved it, the work is complete.

Something is wrong

Tell us through the request form or by replying to your quote. We fix defects in what we delivered at no charge.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method and may take several working days to appear.
