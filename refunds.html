Refunds

Last updated 18 November 2026.

We want you to be happy with what you buy. Here is exactly how refunds work.

In short

Digital files cannot be returned, so we do not refund for change of mind once they are delivered. If what we delivered does not work as described, we fix it, and if we cannot, we refund you. Cancelling before work starts is a full refund.

Template files

Template files are delivered by download link within one working day of payment. If they do not open or do not work as described, tell us within 7 days of delivery. We will fix the problem within 3 working days; if we cannot, we refund the full amount. We do not refund for change of mind or for files that work as described.

Setup & launch

If you cancel before we start work, you get a full refund. If you cancel after work has begun, you pay for the work already done and we refund the rest. Once we have delivered the site and you have approved it, or 7 days pass after delivery without a report of a problem, the work is complete.

Custom build

The quote sets the stages and payments. If you cancel, you pay for the stages already started, and any advance for a stage that has not started is refunded. Work completed and approved is not refundable.

Something is wrong

Tell us through the request form or by replying to your quote. We reply within one working day (Monday to Saturday). We fix defects in what we delivered at no charge for 30 days after delivery.

Payments taken in error

If money was taken from your account but your order was not confirmed, we refund the full amount to the same payment method within 5 to 7 working days. Tell us the payment reference if you do not see it.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the payment method you used, within 5 to 7 working days of approval. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
