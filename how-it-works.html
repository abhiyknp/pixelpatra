How it works

From first look to a live site

Four steps, and you can try everything before you decide.

Explore and try live

Open any template, switch the colour theme, type your own name, and use the Owner view the way you would after launch.

Tell us what you need

Send the short request form: which template, what you want to achieve, your timeline and budget.

Get your quote

We reply within one working day with a clear quote that lists exactly what is included. Nothing starts until you say yes.

We deliver

You receive the template files, or we set the site up with your content and put it live.

Pick a package

Every package starts with the same request form. Prices for each template are on its card.

Template files

from ₹999

  • The complete website files of the template you choose
  • A short written guide to put in your own name, photos and text
  • Everything runs on ordinary hosting, no server needed

Setup & launch

from ₹25,000

  • We add your name, logo, colours, photos and text
  • We connect your domain and put the site live
  • Two rounds of changes after you review it

Custom build

On request

  • Changes to a template's layout or features, or a new page type
  • We agree the scope and price with you in writing first
  • Best for a business with specific needs

What you get after launch

  • An Owner view to change products, prices, text, photos, offers and orders yourself, in the templates that include it.
  • Locked brand details. Your business name and tax details are protected from accidental edits; real changes go through a verified request.
  • A site that works on phones as well as computers.
